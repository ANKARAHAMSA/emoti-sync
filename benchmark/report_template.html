1. Executive Summary & Test Methodology

This evaluation measures the real accuracy of EmotiSync on a class-balanced test set of 921 real-world labeled facial images (up to 150 images per emotion category). Testing was conducted using the identical deep learning models bundled in the app (@vladmandic/face-api 1.7.15) running in an isolated headless WASM environment to ensure absolute parity with in-browser execution.

Core Verdict: The model achieves 60.5% overall accuracy across all 7 emotions (over 4× better than random guessing at 14.3%), and 71.5% accuracy when grouping emotions into Positive, Neutral, and Negative sentiments. Happy (87.7% F1) and Angry (70.3% F1) are highly reliable, while Fearful and Disgusted show lower accuracy and are frequently categorized as Neutral.

4. Confidence Calibration & Reliability Thresholds

The model's raw probability output was analyzed against actual correctness. When the model outputs high confidence (≥90%), its empirical accuracy rises to 70.8%. Conversely, when confidence falls below 70%, accuracy drops to only 37.6%.

Production Guardrail: In the updated application, predictions with confidence under 70% are tagged with an "Unsure" status indicator to maintain user transparency.

Reported Confidence Share of Total Real-World Accuracy

5. Findings & Strategic Recommendations

Key Takeaways for Stakeholder Presentation:

  • Effective for Sentiment & High-Confidence Signals: The system is highly dependable for Positive/Happy (87.7% F1) and Angry/Tension (70.3% F1) detection, achieving 71.5% sentiment polarity accuracy overall.
  • Temporal Multi-Frame Smoothing: Unlike static single-image tests, live webcam analysis utilizes EmotiSync's cross-frame tracking (IoU FaceTracker), preventing momentary flicker and providing a significantly smoother real-time user experience.
  • 100% Privacy & In-Browser Execution: Zero facial images or telemetry data ever leave the client device; all inference executes entirely on-device via WebAssembly/WebGL with bundled weights.
  • Recommended Usage: Ideal for live user sentiment, attention engagement, and mood trend monitoring over time. For strict 7-class forensic classification, confidence filtering (≥70%) should be applied.
